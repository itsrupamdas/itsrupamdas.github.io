GA4 (Google Analytics 4)

Traffic source attribution, custom event modeling, and user journey drop-off analysis.

Overview & Core Application

I configure Google Analytics 4 (GA4) properties to gain granular visibility into cross-channel user behavior, multi-touch attribution, and organic vs. paid acquisition effectiveness.

Step-by-Step Execution Workflow

1

Data Stream & Measurement Setup

Establishing web data streams, enabling enhanced measurement (scrolls, outbound clicks, site search, video engagement), and configuring session timeouts.

2

UTM Parameter Tracking & Campaign Attribution

Structuring systematic UTM tracking taxonomies (source, medium, campaign, content) to attribute Meta, organic, and referral conversions accurately.

3

Custom Exploration Reports & Funnel Analysis

Building Funnel Exploration and Path Exploration reports to uncover high-friction checkout drop-off steps and prioritize CRO fixes.

4

Conversion Goal Modeling

Marking key milestone events as conversions and analyzing acquisition reports to evaluate Channel ROAS.
