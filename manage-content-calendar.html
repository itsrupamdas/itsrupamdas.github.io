Manage Content Calendar

Monthly planning • Strategic scheduling • The 2-Tab Master Spreadsheet Framework.

How I Build the Master Content Calendar

A repeatable 2-tab spreadsheet system to eliminate daily brainstorming and maintain consistent posting.

Tab 1 • Idea Bank: Raw post concepts categorized by content pillars
Tab 2 • Execution Grid: Dated schedule, platform formats & published post URLs
01

Build the Idea Bank by Category

Tab 01 Setup

Brainstorm and organize raw concepts across 4 core pillars (educational, inspirational, entertaining, and promotional) so we never plan a month starting from a blank page.

02

Anchor Holidays & Special Days

Tab 02 • Step A

Mark national holidays and niche awareness dates on the calendar first to prepare timely, contextual posts well in advance.

03

Plan Business Events & Launches

Tab 02 • Step B

Map out company promotions, seasonal launches, and campaign countdowns 2–3 weeks early.

04

Fill Remaining Days from Idea Bank

Tab 02 • Step C

Populate open dates using banked ideas from Tab 1, maintaining a balanced mix of evergreen themes and time-sensitive posts.

05

Assign Channel Formats & Archive Post URLs

Tab 02 • Step D

Specify the format per platform (Reels, carousels, text hooks) and log direct post URLs after publishing to track metrics and performance later.

Template Structure: Built in Google Sheets / Excel with automated category filtering, format tags, and live post URL archiving for easy quarterly reviews.
