Skill Verification • Content Operations

Manage Content Calendar

Monthly planning • Strategic scheduling • The 2-Tab Master Spreadsheet Framework.

Core Philosophy: Proactive Systems Beat Daily Scrambling

A structured content calendar eliminates daily guesswork, protects consistency, and turns chaotic posting into a repeatable 2-Tab Spreadsheet System:

TAB 01

Content Ideas Bank

Centralized idea reservoir organized by 4 key content pillars before touching the calendar.

TAB 02

3-Month Execution Grid

Time-mapped schedule with date anchors, platform rows, content formats, and live URL tracking.

How I Build the Master Content Calendar Template

01 Tab 01 Setup

Build the Idea Bank by Category

Brainstorm and categorize ideas into 4 core pillars before assigning dates:

Informational: Educational tips, how-tos, and industry insights.
Inspirational: Community stories, customer wins, and milestones.
Fun & Entertaining: Relatable humor and behind-the-scenes moments.
Business & Offers: Service updates, product highlights, and promos.
Map Calendar Dates
02 Tab 02 • Step A

Mark Holidays & Special Days

Anchor key dates on the calendar to prepare contextual content in advance:

National Holidays: Mother's Day, Memorial Day, 4th of July, Black Friday.
Niche Awareness Days: Industry-specific celebrations relevant to our audience.
Plan Business Events
03 Tab 02 • Step B

Plan Business Events & Countdowns

Schedule company milestones 2–3 weeks ahead to eliminate daily brainstorming stress:

Launches & Sales: Seasonal promotions, app rollouts, and new services.
Campaign Countdowns: Progressive reminder posts scheduled ahead of deadlines.
Fill Open Days
04 Tab 02 • Step C

Fill Open Days & Classify Content

Populate open dates using Tab 01 ideas, classified by posting flexibility:

Evergreen Content: Timeless posts that can be shifted or refreshed anytime.
Ongoing / Event Content: Time-sensitive posts tied strictly to fixed dates.
Set Platforms & Tracking
05 Tab 02 • Step D

Platform Rows, Formats & Live Tracking

Assign channel rows, format specs, and archive live post URLs:

X / Twitter: Fast text hooks, quotes, and actionable threads.
Instagram: Vertical Reels, swipe carousels, and visual posts.
Facebook: Community discussions, link shares, and video clips.
Post URL Archiving: Direct link pasted after publishing to review metrics later.
