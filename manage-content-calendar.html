Skill Verification • Content Operations

Manage Content Calendar

Goal-first monthly planning, 4-pillar timeless idea banking, STEPPS viral boosters, and the 3-Month Master Excel Calendar system.

Step-by-Step Content Calendar Execution Workflow

01

Goal-First Alignment, Past Performance Audit & Intake

A content calendar is never planned in a vacuum. Before brainstorming topics, I read the client's Social Media Strategy Worksheet (30-day SMART Goal, Primary & Supporting KPIs, Target Audience Personas, and Customer Journey stage) and audit past content performance to see which formats and hooks already resonated.

Goal-Driven Weighting: Follower growth weights heavily on Awareness (50%) & Interest (30%); Sales goals weight heavily on Desire (40%) & Conversion (35%). Performance Audit: Doubling down on proven top-reach formats, high-save carousels, and inbox-driving hooks. Operational Intake: Confirming active platforms, weekly posting days, rest days, realistic team production capacity, and confirmed business dates.
02

Content Ideas Tab • 4-Pillar Timeless Topic Bank

In the Content Ideas tab of the master workbook, I build a timeless topic bank across 4 core categories using a 21-topic menu (tutorials, behind-the-scenes, employee features, customer spotlights, FAQs, UGC, curated articles, and direct product offers) combined with niche research so we never plan a month from a blank page:

Informational: Tutorials, how-to guides, product label/setup checks, website articles, industry data, and curated news with brand opinion. Inspirational: Customer spotlights, transformation/rescue stories, community causes, and milestone thank-you posts. Fun / Entertaining: Relatable audience humor, behind-the-scenes work, team features, comment polls, trivia, and recurring hashtag series. Business: Problem-to-solution product reveals, combo offers, USP breakdowns, and direct purchase CTAs.
03

Intentional Viral Boosters • Jonah Berger’s STEPPS

Not every post should try to go viral — direct selling posts and stock updates stay clear and straightforward. Where the monthly goal requires extra reach, saves, or shares, I selectively apply Jonah Berger’s 6 viral principles (STEPPS) as an intentional booster on standout posts:

Trigger & Public (Brand-Level): Establishing a repeatable series catchphrase/hook and a consistent visual signature (logo placement, brand colors, watermark). Social Currency: Insider tips and niche knowledge that make the sharer look smart and “in the know.” Emotion & Stories: Opening on a heartwarming or relatable moment and wrapping customer/product journeys in a Problem → Turn → Result story. Practical Value: Highly useful, bookmarkable checklists and step-by-step carousels that audiences save and share.
04

Row-by-Row Calendar Grid Mapping & The Rescheduling Rule

While the master Excel template holds a 3-month horizon, I build and deliver one month at a time in a strict row-by-row sequence so date-locked campaigns and flexible evergreen posts stay cleanly separated:

Row 1 • Day/Holiday/Event: Mapping special dates first — Yellow (National Holidays), Pink (Industry Days), Teal (Business Launches/Sales), and Grey (Solemn/Tribute-Only Days with zero selling). Row 2 • Content Focus (Date-Locked): Specific posts created for mapped holidays, sale countdowns, and fixed weekday series that must stay on their exact date. Row 3 • Evergreen/Ongoing (Flexible Buffer): Green for timeless evergreen posts pulled from the Idea Bank and Yellow for repeating weekly routines (e.g., Nightly Question, Weekly Customer Review). The Rescheduling Rule: If production is delayed or an urgent business event arises, date-locked posts stay fixed while green evergreen posts move easily to any open day without breaking the monthly plan.
05

Actionable Platform Formats, Post Details Sheet & Feedback Loop

Instead of generic placeholders, I assign explicit production formats (Reel, 5-Slide Carousel, Infographic, Promo Card) in the Platform rows (Facebook, Instagram) and draft a complete, production-ready specification for every scheduled post in the Post Details sheet:

Complete Post Brief: Date, peak posting time, content pillar, funnel stage, opening 3-second hook, full caption copy, goal-aligned CTA, hashtags, and visual notes. Capacity & Pacing Guardrails: Scheduling strictly within the team’s weekly capacity, keeping rest days blank, and never placing more than 2 posts of the same category in a row. Live Link Archiving & Optimization: Hyperlinking cells to live post URLs once published and logging top-performing hooks into the client profile to optimize the next month’s calendar.
