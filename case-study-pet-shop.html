Case Study 01 • Pet Shop E-Commerce

E-Commerce Website, Content & Catalog Sales Ads

Full-funnel e-commerce setup for a local Pet Shop: mobile-first WordPress storefront & landing pages built via vibe coding, 42-post strategic content calendar executed with AI visuals & videos, Meta Pixel tracking, and Catalog Sales ads.

1. The Business Challenge & Objective

The client operates a physical and online Pet Shop specializing in imported cat food, treats, litter, and travel carriers, but lacked the digital infrastructure to convert social traffic into consistent online orders.

Starting Bottlenecks

  • High Mobile Website Friction: Cluttered storefront and slow cart flow caused mobile shoppers to drop off.
  • Zero Conversion Tracking: No Meta Pixel events to track add-to-carts or purchases.
  • No Structured Content or Ad Creatives: Lacked a monthly posting calendar, video reels, and catalog carousels.

Target Outcome

  • Redesign the WordPress storefront and product landing pages for fast mobile checkout.
  • Plan a 30-day content calendar and produce all post graphics, carousels, and AI video reels.
  • Configure Meta Pixel tracking and launch E-Commerce Catalog Sales campaigns.

2. What Was Delivered (Segment-Wise Outcomes & Proof)
