1. The Business Challenge & Objective

The client operates a physical and online Pet Shop specializing in imported cat food, treats, litter, and travel carriers. While they had strong product inventory, their digital presence lacked the structure needed to convert cold social media traffic into consistent online orders.

Starting Bottlenecks

  • High Website Friction: The existing WordPress store was cluttered and difficult for mobile shoppers to browse or add items to cart.
  • Zero Conversion Tracking: No Meta Pixel or funnel event tracking was in place to measure add-to-carts or purchases.
  • Inconsistent Content & Ad Assets: Lacked a structured monthly posting plan and had no high-converting video reels or product carousels for paid ads.

Month-1 Growth Objective

  • Build a fast, mobile-friendly WordPress storefront and high-converting product landing page.
  • Plan a 30-day content calendar using a structured framework and produce all graphic and video assets using AI.
  • Deploy Meta Pixel tracking and launch structured E-Commerce Catalog & Sales campaigns.

2. End-to-End Execution & Proof

01

Strategic Content Calendar & AI Creative Execution

Strategy + AI Production

I planned the entire monthly content calendar through my own 2-Tab Strategic Framework and brainstorming—mapping out product showcases, cat nutrition tips, authenticity trust seals, and interactive routines. Once the strategy and calendar were locked in, I executed the content production using AI:

  • Framework-Driven Calendar Planning: Brainstormed and scheduled 40+ targeted posts across October & November balancing evergreen education, product rotations, and seasonal sales sprints.
  • Graphic Design & Carousels: Designed branded post graphics, trust-seal banners, and 10-card product comparison carousels.
  • AI Video Generation & Editing: Produced and edited 9:16 vertical AI video ads (including 3D/cartoon space-capsule carrier reels, safe vet trip hooks, and custom voiceovers).

02

WordPress Website & Landing Page Design (Vibe Coding)

Web UX & CRO

To ensure paid ad traffic converts into actual orders instead of bouncing, I rebuilt and customized the client's WordPress/WooCommerce storefront and product landing pages using vibe coding:

  • Mobile-First Storefront Redesign: Engineered clean responsive headers, category grids, and product cards tailored for mobile pet parents.
  • High-Converting Product Landing Pages: Structured dedicated landing views for hero products (10 styles of Cat Travel Carriers & Backpacks) with clear pricing, discount badges, and instant buy actions.
  • Frictionless Slide-Out Mini-Cart & Checkout: Streamlined the cart drawer and Cash-on-Delivery checkout flow so customers can complete orders in seconds.

03

Website Tracking & Meta Pixel Setup

Data & Attribution

Connected the WordPress e-commerce store to Meta's tracking ecosystem so every visitor action is captured accurately for algorithmic ad optimization and retargeting:

  • Standard E-Commerce Funnel Events: Configured and verified PageView, ViewContent, AddToCart, InitiateCheckout, and Purchase events.
  • Catalog Product ID Matching: Ensured product page visits pass dynamic content IDs and values back to Meta Events Manager for dynamic retargeting.

04

E-Commerce Sales & Catalog Ad Campaign

Meta Ads Execution

With the website, landing pages, and pixel ready, I architected and launched structured Meta Ads campaigns designed to drive direct e-commerce catalog sales and high-intent inquiries:

  • 10-Card "Choose Your Style" Catalog Carousel: Mapped 10 distinct pet carrier styles—each with custom pricing headlines, savings tags, and direct product landing page URLs.
  • Multi-Format Creative Testing: Paired 10 standalone static product ads and AI-generated 9:16 vertical video reels to capture both cold impulse buyers and comparison shoppers.
  • Local & Nationwide Audience Structure: Targeted cat parents in high-converting Dhaka zones alongside nationwide Cash-on-Delivery buyers, backed by warm retargeting.

3. Ownership Mindset: Contracted Scope vs. Extra Value Delivered

In the first month, our primary objective was to build the complete marketing and e-commerce foundation within a lean startup budget. Rather than limiting my work to basic ad setup, I took full ownership of the funnel:

Core Committed Deliverables

  • Content Strategy & Posting Plan: Brainstormed and built the master monthly content calendar.
  • Website Tracking Setup: Implemented Meta Pixel and conversion event tracking.
  • Meta Ad Campaign Management: Set up and managed the Facebook & Instagram ad campaigns.

Proactive Extra Value Added

  • Full Website & Mobile UX Redesign (Vibe Coding): Rebuilt the WordPress storefront and landing pages so customers can order effortlessly on mobile.
  • Custom Ad & Post Graphic Design: Designed 20+ static & carousel ad creatives and social posts from scratch.
  • AI Video Ad Production: Scripted, generated, and edited custom 9:16 AI video reels for ads and organic reach.
