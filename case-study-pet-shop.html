Case Study 01 • Pet Shop E-Commerce

E-Commerce Website, Content & Catalog Sales Ads

Full-funnel e-commerce setup for a local Pet Shop: mobile-first WordPress storefront & landing pages built via vibe coding, 42-post strategic content calendar executed with AI visuals & videos, Meta Pixel tracking, and Catalog Sales ads.

1. The Business Challenge & Objective

The client operates a physical and online Pet Shop specializing in imported cat food, treats, litter, and travel carriers, but lacked the digital infrastructure to convert social traffic into consistent online orders.

Starting Bottlenecks

  • High Mobile Website Friction: Cluttered storefront and slow cart flow caused mobile shoppers to drop off.
  • Zero Conversion Tracking: No Meta Pixel events to track add-to-carts or purchases.
  • No Structured Content or Ad Creatives: Lacked a monthly posting calendar, video reels, and catalog carousels.

Target Outcome

  • Redesign the WordPress storefront and product landing pages for fast mobile checkout.
  • Plan a 30-day content calendar and produce all post graphics, carousels, and AI video reels.
  • Configure Meta Pixel tracking and launch E-Commerce Catalog Sales campaigns.

2. What Was Delivered (Segment-Wise Outcomes & Proof)

01

Content Calendar & AI Creative Production

42 Posts • 20+ Graphics • AI Reels
  • 42-Post Monthly Content Calendar: Brainstormed and mapped a 30-day schedule tailored to cat parents (imported food rotations, litter odor solutions, and factory-seal authenticity proof).
  • AI-Executed Visuals & Video Reels: Designed 20+ static & carousel graphics and produced vertical (9:16) AI video ads (3D cartoon capsule carrier animations, UGC lifestyle demos, and custom voiceovers).

03

Website Tracking, GTM & Meta Pixel + Catalog Sync

GTM • Pixel • 96.4% Catalog Match
  • Google Tag Manager & Full-Funnel Pixel Events: Configured GTM (GTM-T58GH6QV) with custom Data Layer variables (content_id, value, currency) to fire and verify PageView, ViewContent, AddToCart, InitiateCheckout, and Purchase events in Meta Events Manager.
  • Commerce Manager Catalog Feed (96.4% Match Rate): Synced the 29-product WooCommerce feed and dedicated 10-Carrier product set directly with the Meta Pixel dataset for dynamic catalog retargeting.

04

E-Commerce Catalog & Pilot Test Ad Campaign (Online + Showroom Impact)

Pilot Test • Online + Walk-In Sales
  • 10-Card "Choose Your Style" Catalog Carousel & Test Funnel: Structured a pilot Sales & Catalog campaign pairing the 10-carrier dynamic carousel, static product cards, and 9:16 AI video reels to test the newly built website and pixel funnel.
  • Online + Physical Showroom Attribution: Because the client operates a local physical showroom in Dhaka and serves predominantly nearby cat parents, the test ads generated initial online orders while driving local pet owners who discovered the products through the ads to walk directly into the shop to inspect and buy in person.
