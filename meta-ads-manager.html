Meta Ads Manager

Full-funnel campaign architecture, CBO/ABO budget scaling, and real-time ROAS optimization on Meta.

Overview & Core Application

I utilize Meta Ads Manager to design, execute, and scale paid acquisition funnels for e-commerce and local service businesses. From objective selection and custom event tracking to algorithmic bidding and multi-creative testing, every setup is structured to maximize conversion volume and lower Cost Per Acquisition (CPA).

Step-by-Step Execution Workflow

1

Funnel Architecture & Campaign Structure

Configuring TOFU (Top of Funnel awareness/prospecting), MOFU (Consideration/engagement), and BOFU (Retargeting) campaigns with clear budget allocation (70/20/10 rule).

2

Audience Targeting & Exclusions

Layering detailed interests, high-affinity lookalikes (1%-3%), and broad targeting while systematically excluding 30-day purchasers to eliminate wasted ad spend.

3

Creative Testing & Dynamic Creative (DCT)

Deploying 3:2:2 dynamic creative testing (3 visuals, 2 primary texts, 2 headlines) to identify winning hooks and scale top-performing creative variations.

4

Optimization & Scaling Rules

Applying automated budget increase rules for winning ad sets with stable ROAS, while pausing underperforming ads past predefined CPA thresholds.
