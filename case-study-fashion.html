1. Client Objective & The Starting Problem

The client sells modern ethnic and western fusion wear for young women (short fotua, stylish kurtis, and contemporary sets). With the Durga Puja shopping season approaching, she was running ads by herself but hit a severe roadblock: messages were costing over ৳10–৳14+ each with low buying intent.

The Bottlenecks

  • Broad Waste: Ads were showing indiscriminately nationwide where modern short fotua has zero cultural market demand.
  • Asset Limitation: Because Puja was already around the corner, the client had no time or budget for an expensive new photoshoot.
  • Ad Fatigue & High CPR: Generic model walk videos and static images failed to hook viewers, driving CPR over ৳13+.

The Growth Objective

  • Cut Cost Per Messaging Conversation below ৳8 BDT.
  • Isolate high-converting urban university/college audiences.
  • Identify the winning creative format through rapid A/B testing and scale it before the Puja festival deadline.

2. Strategic Execution: Cultural Nuance & Creative A/B Testing

1

Cultural & Geographic Audience Segmentation

Bangladeshi cultural fashion differs drastically across regions. Modern short fotua is predominantly worn by university and college students in major urban centers. I geo-fenced targeting strictly to divisional city metropolitan zones and fed contextual student/campus interest signals to Meta’s algorithm, immediately cutting irrelevant impression waste.

2

Creative Diversification (6-Way Format Test in Broad)

Rather than betting the budget on one assumption, I set up a structured creative test with a ৳500/day budget comparing 6 distinct formats:

3

Full-Funnel Retargeting (৳250/day Warm Push)

For prospects who engaged with the top-of-funnel ads but didn't message right away, I launched a dedicated Retargeting Ad Set (৳250 daily budget). Here, I tested product-showing videos and new music variations to close the sale before Puja. The dedicated retargeting ad generated 35 additional messages at ৳6.75 CPR, maximizing return from warm traffic.

4. Strategic Takeaways & Andromeda Creative Diversification

What this campaign proved beyond the numbers:

Native Motion Beats Polished Studio Shoots Slow model walking videos look like traditional ads and are skipped instantly. Fast-paced, rhythmic product transitions immediately showcase the dress details and fabric flow within the first 2 seconds, which dramatically increases ThruPlays and drops CPR.
Scaling Next via Andromeda Creative Diversification To sustain this performance without ad fatigue, I advised the brand to diversify future transition reels using the Andromeda creative framework: showing clothes laid out naturally on green grass, dynamic drop-from-hand reveals, and natural sunlight fabric zooms.
