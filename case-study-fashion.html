1. Client Objective & The Challenge

The client sells modern ethnic fusion wear for young women (short fotua, designer kurtis, and festive sets). With the Durga Puja shopping season approaching, she was running ads by herself but faced two critical bottlenecks:

The Challenge

  • High Ineffective Spend: Messages were costing over ৳10–৳14+ each with low buying intent because ads targeted non-buyers.
  • Content & Time Limitation: With the festival days away, the client had no budget or time for a new professional photoshoot.

The Objective

  • Isolate the right high-intent buyer demographic without ad budget waste.
  • Test available assets (product transition, model walking, carousel, voiceover) to find and scale the winning creative.
  • Lower CPR below ৳8 BDT and close maximum direct sales before Puja.

2. Strategic Execution: Cultural Insight & Creative Iteration

1

Audience Cultural Research & Geographic Filtering

Modern short fotua is not traditionally worn by all women across Bangladesh—it is overwhelmingly preferred by university and college students in urban centers. Based on this cultural insight, I restricted location targeting strictly to major divisional city areas and layered student demographic and campus lifestyle signals to eliminate non-buying audience spend.

2

A/B Creative Testing (Identifying What Works in Practice)

With limited existing assets, I tested 4 distinct visual variations alongside a carousel ad to see what truly resonates:

  • Product Transition Video Ad: Clear top performer. High watch time and engagement drove Meta to direct over 50% of the budget here, delivering 255 messages at ৳6.94.
  • Model Transition Video Ad: Showed strong initial momentum (21 messages at ৳6.67).
  • Model Walking Video & Static Images: Slow walking video (৳12.46 CPR) and static product photos (৳13.41 CPR) cost almost double the transition video, proving fast product motion stops the scroll far better than traditional shoots.
3

Retargeting Strategy with Creative Variations

In the retargeting stage, I created fresh iterations of the winning concept to re-engage warm audiences without fatigue:

  • Produced a product transition version with voiceover highlighting fabric features.
  • Cut the model footage into energetic transition hooks and tested dynamic background music variations.
  • The retargeting ad set generated 57 additional high-intent messages at ৳7.85 CPR (with the product showing ad converting at ৳6.75).

4. Strategic Recommendations & Andromeda Creative Diversification

Key growth conclusions and recommendations provided to the brand for ongoing scaling:

Native Motion Outperforms Traditional Shoots Slow-paced model walking videos mimic traditional studio ads and suffer immediate scroll fatigue. Rhythmic, fast product transitions highlight fabric textures and styling within the opening 2 seconds, which drives higher engagement and significantly lower CPR.
Scaling via Andromeda Creative Diversification To produce high-performing creative variations on a low production budget, I advised the client to film product transitions across varied organic contexts—such as laying fabrics out naturally on green grass, dynamic drop-from-hand reveals, and direct sunlight detail shots. This satisfies Meta's Andromeda algorithm by diversifying creative signals without needing expensive model shoots.
