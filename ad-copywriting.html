Core Skill Verification

Ad Copywriting

Direct-response ad copy frameworks, persuasive hooks, and conversion-focused messaging.

Overview & Core Application

Crafting high-converting ad copy that captures attention in the first 2 lines, articulates value clearly, and drives high-intent clicks to landing pages.

Step-by-Step Execution Workflow

1

Hook Architecture (The First 3 Seconds)

Crafting curiosity-inducing first lines that stop feed scrolling and compel the reader to click 'See more'.

2

Direct-Response Frameworks

Applying PAS (Problem-Agitate-Solve), AIDA (Attention-Interest-Desire-Action), and Before-After-Bridge structures.

3

Objection Handling & Social Proof

Weaving genuine customer testimonials, guarantees, and risk-reversal into body copy to dismantle buyer resistance.

4

Frictionless Call to Action (CTA)

Writing explicit, benefit-oriented CTA commands that set clear expectations for the click destination.
