A/B Testing

Scientific split-testing methodology to isolate variables and systematically reduce CPA.

Overview & Core Application

My framework for testing advertising variables (hooks, angles, formats, audiences) in clean, isolated environments to drive continuous ROAS improvements.

Step-by-Step Execution Workflow

1

Single-Variable Isolation Protocol

Testing only one element at a time (e.g. video hook vs. static image with identical copy) for statistically valid conclusions.

2

Dynamic Creative Testing (DCT)

Using DCT ad sets to find the winning synergy between headlines, visual media, and body copy.

3

Statistical Significance & Winner Extraction

Evaluating results past 50+ conversion events before scaling winners to the primary CBO campaign.

4

Post-Test Implementation

Retiring underperforming creative angles and scaling proven winners into retargeting and lookalike ad sets.
