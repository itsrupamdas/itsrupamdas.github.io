My 4-Step Meta Ads Execution Framework

How I diagnose high acquisition costs, structure testing vs. retargeting budgets, and scale winning creatives.

01

Cultural & Demographic Audience Engineering

Audience Signals

Instead of wasting budget on generic nationwide targeting, I research who actually wears or uses the product in real life. For example, when running ads for women's contemporary short fotua & kurtis during Puja, I identified that this style is primarily worn by university and college students in urban centers—so I restricted geo-targeting strictly to divisional cities and layered student demographic signals.

02

Full-Funnel Budget Architecture (Testing vs. Retargeting)

Budget Control

I split daily budgets strategically between prospecting and closing. In my Festive Fashion campaign, I allocated ৳500/day to Broad Prospecting to test creative hooks at scale, paired with a dedicated ৳250/day Retargeting Ad Set to capture high-intent window shoppers before the festival deadline.

03

Structured Creative A/B Testing (Creative as Targeting)

Hook Testing

When clients have limited assets, I test multiple formats head-to-head: Product Transition Reels, Model Transition Reels, Slow Model Walking Videos, Voiceover Videos, Static Photos, and Carousels. Data proved that fast-paced Product Transition Video Ads (৳6.94 CPR) cut message costs by nearly 50% compared to static photos (৳13.41) and traditional model walks (৳12.46).

04

Retargeting Iteration & Andromeda Creative Diversification

Scaling & Anti-Fatigue

Once a winning concept is found, I adapt it for retargeting by adding product-feature voiceovers, cutting slow model clips into sharp transitions, and testing fresh music hooks (achieving ৳6.75 CPR on warm audiences). To scale long-term without expensive shoots, I guide clients on Meta's Andromeda creative diversification—filming products on natural grass, hand-drop transitions, and varied lighting.
