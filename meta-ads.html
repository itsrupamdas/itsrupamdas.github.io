Meta Ads

Campaign structure, objective selection, budget scaling, cultural audience research, and creative A/B testing.

Overview & Core Application

I design, launch, and optimize full-funnel Meta Ads campaigns across Facebook and Instagram. Rather than relying on guesswork, I combine cultural audience insights with systematic creative diversification (testing product transitions, static visuals, carousels, and voiceovers) and dedicated retargeting to consistently reduce Cost Per Result (CPR).

Step-by-Step Execution Workflow

1. Cultural & Demographic Audience Research

Analyzing real buyer affinity (such as urban university/college students in divisional city zones) to restrict wasted geographic reach and feed high-intent signals to Meta's algorithm.

2. Multi-Format Creative A/B Testing

Testing diverse creative angles simultaneously—fast-paced product transition reels, model walking videos, static images, and carousels—to let real performance data reveal the lowest CPR winner.

3. Full-Funnel Budget Allocation (Broad + Retargeting)

Separating top-of-funnel prospecting (e.g., ৳500/day Broad Audience) from warm bottom-of-funnel retargeting (e.g., ৳250/day Retargeting) with fresh audio and voiceover hooks to close hesitant buyers.

4. Andromeda Creative Diversification

Refreshing winning concepts through varied visual contexts (outdoor grass layouts, hand-drop transitions, feature voiceovers) to scale spend without triggering ad fatigue.
