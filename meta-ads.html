Meta Ads

Andromeda creative-led funneling • ABO budget control • Winning ad iteration & audience signal research.

How I Structure & Scale Meta Ads

My 4-step system built for Meta's Andromeda algorithm to lower cost per result and convert warm buyers.

01

Creative-Led Funnel & Sales Objective (Andromeda Update)

Funnel Architecture

With Meta’s Andromeda update, I keep the campaign goal focused on Sales / Conversions so Meta can funnel users independently based on how they interact with each creative format:

TOF • Top of Funnel Product Transition & Model Ads Fast-paced motion hooks cold audiences and stops the scroll immediately.
MOF • Middle of Funnel Voiceover Feature Ads Voiceover explaining product fabric, fit, and features builds buyer trust.
BOF • Bottom of Funnel Carousel Ads with Offer Multi-card product showcase with a clear offer converts warm window shoppers.
02

Ad Set Budget Control (ABO) for Guaranteed Retargeting

Budget Strategy

Instead of letting campaign-level budget dump 100% of spend into cold traffic, I use Ad Set Level Budgets (ABO). This guarantees a dedicated daily budget specifically to retarget interested audiences (video viewers, page engagers, and message openers) alongside the broad prospecting ad set.

03

Winning Ad Iteration & Creative Diversification

A/B Testing & Scaling

Once testing reveals the winning ad format (e.g., Product Transition Video), I don't guess from scratch—I build targeted variations of the winner: cutting slow model clips into sharp transitions, layering voiceover details, changing music hooks, and applying Andromeda creative diversification (showing products on grass, hand-drop transitions) to scale without ad fatigue.

04

Cultural & Demographic Audience Signal Research

Audience Signals

Before launching, I research cultural buying habits to feed clean signals to Meta. For example, recognizing that modern short fotua/kurtis are primarily worn by university and college students in urban areas, I restrict location targeting to divisional cities and provide relevant student/lifestyle keyword suggestions to prevent wasted impressions.
