Core Skill Verification

Creative Diversification

Multi-format creative production to eliminate ad fatigue and maintain scale.

Overview & Core Application

Developing a diverse portfolio of video, carousel, static, and social proof creatives to appeal to different buyer stages and avoid audience ad fatigue.

Step-by-Step Execution Workflow

1

Format Variety Allocation

Balancing UGC video reels (for attention), carousel product breakdowns (for consideration), and static price cards (for conversion).

2

Hook Diversity

Developing 5 distinct psychological hooks per product: Problem-Aware, Social Proof, Curiosity, Direct Benefit, and Us vs. Them.

3

Ad Fatigue Monitoring

Tracking frequency metrics and first-time impression ratios to refresh creative batches before CPA spikes occur.

4

Iterative Creative Refresh

Remixing top-performing video hooks with new B-roll and updating headline overlays to extend winning ad lifespan.
